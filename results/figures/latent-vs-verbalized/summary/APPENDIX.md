# Appendix one-liners (stays out of the summary) — each with its raw

All under `results/raw/local-monitor-activations/` unless a path is given; sections are in `experiments/local-monitor-activations/RESULTS.md` (G track) or `experiments/latent-vs-verbalized/RESULTS.md`.

- **G1–G3 environment and attention.** Blackwell sm_120 pod, pinned packages, gpt-oss-20b loads at 12.83 GiB; neither shipped attention path runs 25k-token prompts (eager O(L²), flex fails at decode), so a chunked eager attention (`chunked_attention.py`, chunk 256) was written — same arithmetic as eager except fp32 sink softmax, which accounts for the model-level logit gap. Raws `g1_env_20260910T070739Z.json`, `g1_attn_probe_20260910T071901Z.json`, `g1_attn_probe2_20260910T074025Z.json`, `g1_attn_unit_20260910T075628Z.json` (G1, 2026-09-10).
- **Chunk-size noise floor.** Chunk 256 vs 128 on the same 20 actions: |ΔE| mean 0.058, max 0.229; last-layer cosine distance 0.001–0.007 median, about 1/30 of the between-action distance at content positions, so any E gap below ~0.1 is inside the floor. Raw `g3_pilot_20260910T111233Z.json` (`.noise_floor`; G3.2, 2026-09-10).
- **The halted gate and the ordering story.** The 200-action gate fired (3.0 % ≥ 3, sd E 0.187) but the first 200 actions in ascending-length order were opening steps of 200 trajectories (median step 1); on the 105 consistent-high actions the same monitor scores 3.93 vs 0.76 (Mann–Whitney p = 2e-16), so the halt was an ordering artifact, not flatness. Raws `g4_gate_20260910T133150Z.json`, `g4_gate_report_20260910T133254Z.json`, `g3_consistent_high_report_20260910T142321Z.json`, `g4_consistent_high_regression_20260910T211741Z.json` (bitwise re-run) (2026-09-10).
- **Reasoning: medium and k = 4 samples.** On ten actions greedy medium gives 1 on 9/10 (verdicts collapse to a constant, +21 s/action); four T = 1 samples restore variance but not ranking (Spearman vs cached −0.03; +29 h projected). Raw `g3_variants_20260910T122844Z.json` (G3 addendum 3, 2026-09-10).
- **Placeholder-rule decomposition.** The join reproduces every label-branch gate number to ±0.01 under both placeholder rules; attack 347 flagged / 74 current-action / 273 history-only, honest 27 / 13 / 14; the 13 honest current-action placeholders come from one trajectory and score ≥ 3 at 0.615; the appendix run with placeholders included moves every P3 number by < 0.01. Raws `results/raw/latent-vs-verbalized/p0_gate_20260911T142420Z.json` (`.placeholder_counts`, `.split_side_x_placeholder`), `p3_baselines_phnone_20260911T144124Z.json` (2026-09-11).
- **17,234 vs 12,494.** The export manifest holds 17,234 actions; the paper's cached-monitor pool is 12,494 honest actions (92 attack trajectories); `subset_main` is 2,012 actions from 59 trajectories after the 32k-token cap, which drops 27 % of attack and 52 % of honest actions and keeps the early part of every trajectory. Raws `g2_prep_20260910T101555Z.json` (`.cap_projection`), `results/raw/logit-curve-replication/l3_logit_roc_*.json` (the 12,494 pool) (G2.5, 2026-09-10).
- **MXFP4 evidence.** `Mxfp4HfQuantizer` with `dequantize == False`; expert tensors read off the module as `FloatType(e2m1)` with uint8 scales; 12.83 GiB allocated where bf16 would need ~40 GB; asserted at load in every later raw (`model.mxfp4_native`). Raw `g1_env_20260910T070739Z.json` (G1.5, 2026-09-10).
